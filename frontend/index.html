Community Awareness

What is rainwater harvesting?

Collecting rain from rooftops and storing it for later use, instead of letting it run away.

Benefits

Saves water

Less need for borewell, tanker or piped water.

Saves money

Lower water bills and fewer tanker trips.

Helps the ground

Extra water can refill groundwater and reduce flooding.

Useful in dry months

Stored water is ready when supply is short.

Water-saving tips

  • Fix leaking taps and pipes quickly.
  • Turn off the tap while brushing or washing.
  • Use stored rainwater for plants, cleaning and washing.
  • Water plants early morning or evening.
  • Keep the tank lid closed to stop mosquitoes and dirt.
  • Share these tips with neighbours and family.

Rainwater is not safe to drink unless it is filtered and treated first.

About RAINWISE

RAINWISE – Intelligent Rainwater Harvesting Monitoring and Decision Support System – is a community service project that helps people use rainwater wisely.

How it works

  1. An ESP32 board reads a rain sensor and a water-level sensor (HC-SR04) on the tank.
  2. It sends the readings over Wi-Fi to the RAINWISE server.
  3. The server saves them in a database and works out level, litres and advice.
  4. This website shows the results in simple form.

Good to know

The rain sensor shows how wet it is (none, light, heavy). It does not measure millimetres of rain. Use the Calculator with rainfall from a weather report or rain gauge.

When no hardware is connected, the Dashboard and Tips show demo data, clearly labelled.
